Full Curriculum & Details

Pre-Primary & Primary (Nursery–V)

Joyful activity-based foundation focusing on phonics, reading, numeracy, and confident communication.

High School (Classes VI – X)

Concept-driven State Board / SSC curriculum with weekly slip tests, CCE reviews & Board exam mastery.

IIT-JEE & NEET Foundation

Structured Maths, Physics, Chemistry & Biology foundation with weekly OMR & CBT mock tests from Class VI.

Navodaya & Olympiads

Special coaching for JNVST, Sainik School entrance, Mental Ability, and National Science & Maths Olympiads.
